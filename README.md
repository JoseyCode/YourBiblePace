# YourBiblePace

## What is YourBiblePace?
*YourBiblePace* is an Android app created to help people read their Bibles (all of it). It's built to work around life and its messiness and ultimately - it's built for *you*. If you're someone like me who has struggled for years to read their whole Bible, I strongly suggest giving my app a try.

## Why did I create YourBiblePace?
I created *YourBiblePace* because I grew frustrated at my own ability to read the entirety of my Bible. Far too often I would resolve to read my Bible, only to be active for a few months, then ultimately come to a halt. I've used plans. I've used tracking apps. I've used it all, but despite that, I never made it to the other end of the cover. *That is the core issue.* Myself (and many of the people I know) operate with an all or nothing mentality. I either read it all, or I read none of it! Yet that's not the way life works. Life happens, kids happens, sometimes you just need a break and that's okay. The goal isn't to be perfect, the goal is to make progress.
